How EJG-OS Pricing Works

Every install starts with the $1,000 Base Package — that's your EJG-OS hub, wall tablet, 3 Zooz Z-Wave switches, and your professional setup. You then add the exact devices your home needs at transparent retail prices, each one already including its installation. No monthly fees, no cloud lock-in.
Base Package — $1,000
  • ✓ EJG-OS Local Hub (Raspberry Pi with SSD)
  • ✓ 10" wall-mount tablet (Home dashboard)
  • ✓ 3 Zooz Z-Wave switches, installed
  • ✓ Professional installation + first-day tuning
  • ✓ Secure remote access — your own private subdomain
  • ✓ Free lifetime software updates

Why the switches? We don't install a hub with nothing attached to it — three devices is where a working smart home starts, so they go in with every system.

Add cameras? Every camera install requires a dedicated network video recorder (NVR) with its own storage drive. Cameras record to the NVR — not to your hub — so footage stays inside your home and the hub stays fast for everything else. The NVR is a TRENDnet TV-NVR1508 (8-channel 4K, 8 PoE ports) at $239, plus a WD Purple surveillance drive sized by how many days you want to keep (from $199). PoE cameras plug straight into the NVR's PoE ports, which powers them and keeps them on their own isolated camera network with no path to your home Wi-Fi or the internet. The indoor pan/tilt camera is a Wi-Fi unit — it joins a separate camera SSID and records to the same NVR. Past 8 cameras a PoE switch ($300) powers and connects the rest, and the builder adds it automatically. None of this is bundled for builds without cameras.

Camera Recording & Storage

Cameras record continuously to the NVR's drive, and the NVR overwrites the oldest footage once the drive is full. Pick a drive by how many days of history you want on hand. Live and recorded video is viewed through the same EJG-OS app as the rest of your home.

Warranty

Hardware — 12 months

The hardware is warranted against manufacturing defects for 12 months from the date of installation. If a device fails because of a defect in how it was made, we deal with it.

Not covered: damage from improper operation or use of the product, and damage caused by moving the product after it has been installed.

Workmanship — 12 months

Separate from the hardware warranty, and worth knowing the difference. The hardware warranty covers a device that fails. The workmanship warranty covers the installation itself.

For 12 months, if something was mounted, wired or configured incorrectly on our end, we come back and put it right at no charge.

After the install

Changes or additions outside the original installation scope are charged at the current service rate, or covered by the $30/month Priority Support plan.

EJG-OS is a lifestyle and convenience product. It is not sold or warranted as a monitored security system.

Pricing FAQs

Are there any hidden fees? ▼
No. The price you see is the price you pay. Installation, hardware, configuration, and training are all included. The only optional add-on is a Priority Support plan at $30/month — no contract, cancel anytime.
Can I upgrade my system later? ▼
Yes! You can add devices and features anytime. Your installer can expand your system as your needs grow. There's no contract locking you into anything.
What's included in the installation fee? ▼
Installation includes: hardware purchase, professional setup, Z-Wave hub configuration, device pairing, dashboard customization, scene setup, training, and full handoff. Everything needed to go from zero to fully operational.
What about device costs beyond the quoted hardware? ▼
The quoted price includes hardware. If you want to expand beyond the base package, your installer will quote additional devices. You control what gets installed and when.
What do cameras need beyond the cameras themselves? ▼
A dedicated network video recorder (NVR) with its own storage drive. The NVR is required for every camera install: cameras record to it around the clock, the footage stays inside your home, and your EJG-OS hub is left free to run lights, scenes and voice without competing for resources. PoE cameras plug directly into the NVR's built-in PoE ports, which powers them and keeps them on their own isolated camera network with no path to your home Wi-Fi or the internet. The indoor pan/tilt camera is a Wi-Fi unit on a separate camera SSID, and records to the same NVR. Past 8 cameras a PoE switch ($300) powers and connects the rest; the builder adds it automatically. You choose the drive size by how many days of footage you want to keep (see the storage table above).
Is the support plan really optional? ▼
100% optional. Every install includes ongoing support from your installer. The Priority Support plan is a $30/month add-on for homeowners who want priority response and hands-on help configuring new devices or automations later. Add it anytime, cancel anytime.
