EJG-OS vs Alexa and Google Home

Alexa and Google Home are do-it-yourself platforms built around Amazon and Google accounts. EJG-OS is installed for you and runs on its own hub. We don't connect Alexa or Google Home to EJG-OS.

Side by side

We compare ownership, local operation, fees, installation, openness and privacy. Statements about Amazon Alexa and Google Home are what the company itself publishes, listed under Sources. Plans vary, and details change, so check the company before you buy.

TopicAmazon Alexa and Google HomeEJG-OS
Who owns the hardwareYou buy Echo and Google devices. Both are used with an account: Google states a Google Account is required to set up Matter devices in Google Home, and Amazon's pages refer to the devices connected to your Alexa account.You own the hardware once you have paid in full. The EJG-OS software is licensed for the hub we install, for your household.
Works without the internetAmazon states Echo Hub can control select devices, such as lights, switches and plugs, even if the internet is down. Google states Matter devices are designed to work locally over the home network, and that commands from away from home are carried out through the cloud.Lights, locks, heating, scenes and automations run on the hub in your home and keep working with the internet unplugged. Remote access, phone alerts and the optional weather and music extras need the internet.
Monthly fees and contractGoogle Home Premium is a paid subscription with Standard and Advanced plans, billed monthly or annually. Plans and prices vary, so check each company's current pricing.No required subscription. A one-time installed price (a $1,000 Base Package plus devices at flat installed prices, in CAD before GST). Priority Support is optional at $30/month plus GST, month to month.
InstallationSetup is done by you in the Alexa or Google Home app.Professionally installed and set up by EJG in Calgary, Airdrie, Cochrane, Okotoks, Chestermere and Springbank.
Platform opennessEcho Hub lists support for Zigbee, Sidewalk, Thread, Bluetooth and Matter. Amazon and Google both document ways for other manufacturers to connect, including Matter and cloud-based integrations.Z-Wave devices, with S2 encryption on supported devices. EJG-OS is licensed software, not open source. We don't connect Alexa or Google Home.
PrivacyEach company publishes its own privacy terms for its account and services. Read them for how voice and home data are handled.Your cameras, recordings, device history and settings are stored on the hub in your home, not in the cloud. We don't keep copies on our servers. Phone alerts pass through Apple, Google and Expo, and optional services you turn on use their own providers.

Which fits what

  • If you want a low-cost start and are happy setting things up yourself, Alexa and Google Home are widely available.
  • If you want a hub in your house that runs your lights, locks and heating on its own, installed and set up for you, that is what EJG-OS is for.
