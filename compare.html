Comparisons

EJG-OS vs Telus / ADT smart home

Telus and EJG-OS are built for different jobs. Telus SmartHome Security is sold with professional monitoring plans. EJG-OS is a home automation system you own, installed in your house. EJG-OS is not an alarm or monitoring service and does not replace one.

EJG-OS vs Control4

Control4 and EJG-OS are both professionally installed home automation systems. The differences below are the ones each company states publicly.

EJG-OS vs Home Assistant

Home Assistant and EJG-OS both run in your home. The main difference is who builds and maintains it: Home Assistant is free software you set up yourself, and EJG-OS is installed and set up for you.

EJG-OS vs Alexa / Google DIY

Alexa and Google Home are do-it-yourself platforms built around Amazon and Google accounts. EJG-OS is installed for you and runs on its own hub. We don't connect Alexa or Google Home to EJG-OS.
