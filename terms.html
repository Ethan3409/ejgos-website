Your quote lists the EJG-OS hub, the wall tablet and mount, the Z-Wave devices and any cameras, recorder and storage you chose, plus installation, configuration, first-day tuning and a walkthrough of the system. Anything not on the quote is not included. Additions later are quoted separately.

EJG-OS is a convenience and lifestyle product. It is self-monitored: it tells you when something happens. There is no monitoring centre, no one is dispatched on your behalf, and it cannot guarantee that anything is prevented. In an emergency call 911.

3. Installation and what we need from you

We agree an installation date with you. Most lighting-and-lock installs are done in one day; homes with cameras take longer, and we give you a schedule at the consultation. Dates are our best estimate, not a guarantee, and we tell you promptly if one changes.

You provide safe access to the home and the work areas, working mains power at the locations that need it, and a wired internet connection for the hub (EJG-OS connects by Ethernet). Where an existing wall box, wiring or panel is not suitable for the device you chose, we tell you before we proceed and quote what is needed.

4. Ownership, software and your data

  • Hardware. The hardware on your quote becomes yours when you have paid for it in full.
  • Software. The EJG-OS software is licensed to your home, not sold. You may use it on the hub we install, for your household, for as long as you keep that hub. You may not copy, resell or redistribute it.
  • Your data. The information about your home that lives on your hub (devices, history, scenes, automations, recordings) is yours. We do not sell it. See the Privacy Policy for what leaves your home and why.

5. Warranty

  • Not covered: damage from improper operation or use, damage caused by moving a product after installation, damage from power surges, flooding, fire or other events outside our control, wear and tear on batteries and consumables, and faults in equipment we did not supply or install.
  • Manufacturer warranties. Third-party devices (for example locks, thermostats, cameras and drives) also carry their manufacturers’ warranties. Where a manufacturer’s warranty lasts longer than ours, we help you make a claim under it.

6. Software updates and support period

EJG-OS software updates are free while your hub runs a current EJG-OS release. We tell you in writing if a version stops being supported, and we give you time to update before it does. Updates are digitally signed and delivered to your hub by your installer. Changes to your own system’s setup (new devices, rooms, scenes or automations built by your installer) are quoted as service, or covered by the Priority Support plan.

Services we operate, such as remote access through EJG Secure Link, depend on EJG continuing to run them. If we ever stop, the lights, locks, scenes and automations on your hub keep working at home; remote access and updates would stop until another provider took them over.

You can switch the health report off in the app. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
