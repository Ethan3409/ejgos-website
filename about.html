About

EJG Technologies Inc.

We build and install EJG-OS, a smart home system that runs on hardware in your house. Based in Calgary, Alberta.

You already work for your home. Let it work for you.
Ethan Fischer, founder, EJG Technologies Inc.

Everywhere you go, you are doing something. Groceries, work, appointments, one thing after another. Home should be the one place that doesn't ask more of you.

EJG-OS is a smart home system that runs on a hub installed in your home. Lights, locks, heating, cameras and scenes are controlled from a wall-mounted tablet, from your phone, or by voice through Skip, the voice assistant built into the system.

It runs locally. The system keeps working with the internet unplugged, your home's data stays on your hub, and you own the hardware outright. No subscription is required to use it.

EJG Technologies Inc. is registered in Alberta and installs in Calgary and the surrounding area. Installation is included in the price of every device.

What happens if EJG Technologies Inc. is no longer around?

It's a fair question to ask of any small company selling something this permanent, and it deserves a straight answer rather than a reassuring one.

Your smart home does not depend on EJG Technologies Inc. still being here. That isn't a promise about anyone's plans. It's how the system is built:

  • You own the hardware outright. The hub, the tablet, the switches, the cameras. They're yours from the day they're installed. Nothing is leased, financed, or licensed back to you.
  • It runs entirely in your house. Your scenes, schedules and automations execute on the hub in your home. There's no account to expire, no remote server to go offline, and no subscription required to keep any of it working. Unplug the internet and your home still works.
  • There's no lock-in to undo. The devices are standard Z-Wave hardware, and the hub is ordinary hardware running a documented setup. If you ever want a different installer, nothing proprietary stands in the way. They can take it over.
  • Nothing of yours is held anywhere else. Your data lives on your hub. There's no export to request and no account to recover, because there was never a copy off your property to begin with.

What you would lose is the support that comes with the install. That's a real loss and worth weighing. What you would not lose is the system you paid for, or the ability to have someone else look after it.
