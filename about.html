Our Story

Built Out of Frustration. Designed for Freedom.

One person got tired of paying rent on their own home. Monthly for the thermostat. Subscriptions for cameras. Apps that died when companies got acquired. So they built something different.

The Why

Smart home became a rental agreement in disguise.

When you buy a car, you own it. You pay once, drive it, maintain it, sell it when you're done. But somehow "smart home" became different. Monthly fees for a thermostat you bought. A subscription to see your own security cameras. Apps that evaporated when the company got acquired, taking your automations with them. You didn't own your home — you rented access to it.

The worst part? It didn't have to be this way. The technology is simple. Local hardware can do everything you need. But the business model demanded subscriptions. Data harvesting. Lock-in. Cloud dependency. Because that's where the money is for venture-backed startups.

"You shouldn't need a password and a subscription to turn on your own lights."

That's where EJG-OS came from. What if you could own your smart home the way you own your car? You buy it. You own it. It works. No required monthly bill. No company deciding what features you get access to. No data being harvested. Just your house, doing what you told it to do, forever.

We built it in Calgary because that's home — and because Calgary winters force you to build things right. When your furnace integration matters, when keeping your home comfortable in -30°C is real, you don't cut corners. You build something that genuinely works, every single time.

Here's the core idea: a smart home OS that runs entirely on hardware inside your house. Your automations work even when the internet's down. Your camera footage never leaves your walls. Your data is yours alone. Every install includes a dedicated EJG-OS tablet mounted in the home as the control hub — this isn't some optional feature, it's standard. The person who designs your system is the person who installs it and the person who picks up the phone a year later. Nothing is subcontracted. Then it's yours. Forever.

We built on Z-Wave — a rock-solid, dedicated-frequency mesh protocol — because we wanted your system to work with thousands of different devices without depending on Wi-Fi or the cloud. Not a locked ecosystem. Not a proprietary garden. Real choice about what hardware you use.

This isn't a startup pitch. It's the opposite of that. We're not trying to build the next unicorn or get acquired. We're building something that actually works for people who live in homes. EJG-OS solves a real problem: the feeling that you should be able to control your own house without paying rent to a company forever.

What We Actually Believe

Not marketing values. Real principles that show up in every decision.

🏠

You Actually Own It

One price. Professional install. Then it's yours. Like buying a house instead of renting. No monthly fees hiding in your account. No subscriptions expiring. No company deciding which features you get to keep. It's paid for. It's running. It's done.

🔒

Your Data Stays in Your House

Your home's patterns, your cameras, your device history — all stored and processed on the hub in your house, on hardware you own. We don't run a cloud that copies it, and we can't reach your hub unless you ask us to. When you use remote access it's end-to-end encrypted between your hub and your phone, so no third party in the middle can read it. The one exception is the optional “Hey Skip” voice assistant, which sends what you say to an external language model to understand it — our privacy policy spells that out. Everything else stays home.

⚡

Everything Runs Locally

Your commands don't travel to a server farm and back. The EJG-OS hub installed in your home processes everything right there — the wall tablet is just one of the screens that talks to it. Instant response. Unplug your internet and your lights, locks, scenes and schedules keep working. No dependency on a cloud company staying in business or keeping its prices reasonable. Your home operates independently.

🤝

Straight Talk, No Upsell

We tell you what you actually need, not what makes us more money. No hidden fees in fine print. No convincing you to buy features you won't use. If a simpler system works for your home, that's what we'll recommend. Trust is earned by being honest, especially when honesty costs us.

🇨🇦

Built for This Country

We built this in Alberta. We know what Canadian winters demand. We understand privacy expectations in Canada. We respect Canadian standards and regulations. This isn't a California system retrofitted for our market — it was built here, for here, by people who actually live here.

📱

Your Tablet, Your Hub

Every home gets a dedicated EJG-OS tablet mounted where you need it — the control center for everything. It's not optional. It's standard. A real, physical interface in your home that you own, that doesn't depend on a smartphone app, that's always there and always yours.

“What happens if you're not around?”

It's a fair question to ask anyone buying something this permanent from one person, and it deserves a straight answer rather than a reassuring one.

The honest version is that your smart home doesn't depend on me still being here. That isn't a promise about my plans — it's how the system is built:

  • You own the hardware outright. The hub, the tablet, the switches, the cameras — they're yours from the day they're installed. Nothing is leased, financed, or licensed back to you.
  • It runs entirely in your house. Your scenes, schedules and automations execute on the hub in your home. There's no account to expire, no server of mine to go offline, and no subscription required to keep any of it working. Unplug the internet and your home still works.
  • There's no lock-in to undo. The devices are standard Z-Wave and Wi-Fi hardware, and the hub is ordinary hardware running a documented setup. If you ever want a different installer, there's nothing proprietary standing in the way — they can take it over.
  • Nothing of yours is held anywhere else. Your data lives on your hub. There's no export to request and no account to recover, because there was never a copy off your property to begin with.

What you'd lose if I stopped doing this is me — someone who knows your house, answers the phone, and can change things quickly. That's a real loss and I won't pretend otherwise. What you would not lose is the system you paid for, or your ability to have someone else look after it.

The flip side of being one person is the reason the work is consistent: the person who designs your system is the person who installs it, and the person who picks it up again a year later. Nothing is handed to a subcontractor who's never seen your house.

How This Actually Happened

Not a typical startup story. Just what actually occurred.

The Real Problem

Tired of paying rent on home tech

Monthly bill for the thermostat. Cloud subscription for the cameras. An app that stopped working when the company got acquired. Realizing: I own this house, but I rent the ability to control it. That's broken.

The Search

Nothing existed that actually solved this

Every existing option either needed a computer science degree to set up, locked you into one brand, or still depended on cloud servers (defeating the purpose). There genuinely wasn't an alternative — a local-first smart home OS you could own outright and have installed by a professional.

The Build

Built it. In Calgary. During winter.

Open protocols. Local processing. Real hardware tablet for control. Professional installation standard from day one. Calgary winters meant no cutting corners — your heating needs to work when it's -30°C. That forces you to build things right.

First Installs

Real homes, real people, real feedback

Started installing in Calgary with families willing to try something different. Each install taught us what actually matters. Not what investors want to hear — what homeowners actually need.

Now

Owner-operated, and opening up

Every EJG-OS install to date is done by the person who built it. That's why the work is consistent: one set of hands, one standard, no subcontractors. The next step is bringing on other installers who believe the same thing — that people should genuinely own their homes. Not a tech company pretending to care about privacy. Actually building it.

Our mission is simple: your home, fully intelligent, completely yours.

No required subscription. No cloud storage of your home's data. Just a home that works exactly the way you want it to — and keeps working with the internet unplugged. Priority Support is there if you want it, and only if you want it.
