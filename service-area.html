What an install looks like

The same process applies in every town. We start with a free consultation about your home and what you want it to do. We then quote your home in writing, install the hub, tablet and devices, set everything up around how your household lives, and walk you through it. How it works has the five steps.

Most lighting-and-lock installs are done in one day. Homes with cameras take longer; we give you a schedule at the consultation.

What a Calgary-area home needs

  • Working mains power at the locations that need it.
  • A wired internet connection for the hub, because EJG-OS connects by Ethernet.
  • Access to the work areas on install day.

If an existing wall box, wiring or panel is not suitable for a device you chose, we tell you before we proceed and quote what is needed.

What it costs

Every system starts with a $1,000 Base Package, and devices are added at flat installed prices. Sample packages are $1,512, $3,483 and $6,021, all in Canadian dollars before GST. See pricing or the custom builder.
