Everything a smart home should be able to do.

Features built for privacy and local control. Lights, locks, heating, scenes and automations keep running with the internet unplugged.

What EJG-OS Does

Personalized Dashboard

Pin your most-used devices to the home screen. Personalized greetings by name. Your home, your way.

Room-by-Room Control

Organize all devices by room for intuitive control. See status at a glance. Tap to interact.

Smart Scenes

One-tap scenes for Good Morning, Away, Movie, Sleep. Set several devices at once, instantly.

Automations Engine

Time-based, sunrise/sunset, motion-based, and condition-based automations. They run locally on your hub.

Cameras

Cameras record to a dedicated NVR in your home (required for every camera install); PoE cameras plug into its ports on their own isolated camera network. No cloud video subscription, and we turn off the recorder's cloud features. Recordings stay on the recorder in your home. Ask us about viewing your cameras in the EJG-OS app.

Voice Control "Hey Skip"

Built-in voice assistant. No smart speaker to buy. No subscriptions. Control your home with your voice. Skip can be turned off, and it needs an internet connection today.

Climate Control

Thermostat setpoints and schedules from the app, the tablet, scenes and automations.

Smart Locks & Security

Lock and unlock from the app. Keypad codes for family and guests, including temporary and one-time codes. Alerts for lock and door events. Self-monitored: alerts come to you, not to a monitoring service.

Device Health Monitoring

See when a device goes offline. Keep your system healthy.

Sleep Screen Mode

Minimal ambient display for wall-mounted tablets. Dimmed at night. Always available.

Local-First Privacy

Your device history, scenes and automations are stored and processed on the hub in your house, not on our servers. No usage data sold. Remote access is encrypted in transit, and homes on EJG Secure Link get end-to-end encryption between the hub and your phone. A few optional features use outside services: Skip uses Apple's or Google's speech service, can send requests to an outside AI service (Anthropic's Claude), and can use OpenAI for its voice; phone alerts pass through Apple and Google as a plain “you have a new alert” notice, with the details opened from your hub in the app. Our privacy policy names every one.
