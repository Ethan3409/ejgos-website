Warranty & Support

What every install comes with, and the optional Priority Support plan.

Warranty

Hardware — 12 months

The hardware is warranted against manufacturing defects for 12 months from the date of installation. If a device fails because of a defect in how it was made, we deal with it.

Not covered: damage from improper operation or use of the product, and damage caused by moving the product after it has been installed.

Workmanship — 12 months

Separate from the hardware warranty, and worth knowing the difference. The hardware warranty covers a device that fails. The workmanship warranty covers the installation itself.

For 12 months, if something was mounted, wired or configured incorrectly on our end, we come back and put it right at no charge.

After the install

Free software updates to EJG-OS. Changes to your own system's setup — new devices, rooms or scenes built by your installer — are quoted as service, or covered by the $30/month Priority Support plan.

Security on EJG-OS is self-monitored

The security hardware is real: cameras, smart locks, door and window contacts, motion and glass-break sensors, and an optional siren. It records and reports what happens.

What it does with an event is tell you. Alerts go to your phone and to your wall tablet. There is no monitoring centre, nobody else is watching your cameras, and no one is dispatched on your behalf. EJG-OS will not call the police or the fire department, and it cannot guarantee that anything is prevented. It reports and it records. Deciding what to do next is yours.

EJG-OS is a lifestyle and convenience product, not a monitored security system.

This is a summary. Ask us about anything here before you book.

Optional Priority Support Plan

Every install comes with a 12-month hardware warranty and a 12-month workmanship warranty. The Priority Support plan is an optional add-on for homeowners who want priority response, secure remote diagnostics into your hub, and hands-on help configuring new devices or automations.

$30/month. No contract, cancel anytime.

Questions about your warranty or support? Every enquiry is answered within one business day. Contact us.
