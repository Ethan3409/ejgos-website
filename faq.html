Getting started

What is EJG-OS?

EJG-OS is a professionally installed smart home system for Calgary-area houses. A hub in your home runs your lights, locks, heating and automations, and they keep working with the internet unplugged. You control them from a wall-mounted tablet, from the app on your phone, or by voice through Skip. There is no required subscription.

Do I need to be technical to use it?

No. Everything is set up and configured for you during the install, and tuned on the first day so it works the way your household actually lives. Day to day you use the wall tablet or the EJG Home app: tap a scene, open a lock, check the thermostat. You can run scenes, switch automations on or off and manage lock codes in the app; new devices, rooms, scenes and automations are set up by your installer, so nothing asks you to learn a configuration file.

How long does installation take?

Most lighting-and-lock installs are done in one day. Homes with cameras take longer; we give you a schedule at the consultation.

What do I need at home before the install?

Access to the work areas, working mains power at the locations that need it, and a wired internet connection for the hub, because EJG-OS connects by Ethernet. If an existing wall box, wiring or panel is not suitable for a device you chose, we tell you before we proceed and quote what is needed.

Is the consultation free?

Yes. You will be talking to the person who does the install, and every enquiry is answered within one business day. You can request a consultation or call (587) 209-3608.

Where do you install?

We install in Calgary, Airdrie, Cochrane, Okotoks, Chestermere and Springbank, and the surrounding area. See the service area page, or ask us about your address.

Pricing and warranty

How much does EJG-OS cost?

Every system starts with the $1,000 Base Package: the hub, a 10-inch wall-mounted control tablet with its frame and mount, three Zooz Z-Wave switches, professional installation and first-day tuning. Devices are added at flat installed prices. Sample packages are $1,512 (Essentials, 10 devices in total), $3,483 (Standard Home, 31 devices) and $6,021 (Premium, 60 devices). Prices are installed prices in Canadian dollars, before GST. Use the custom builder to price your own home.

Do I need to pay a monthly fee?

There is no required subscription. The base EJG-OS platform is a one-time installation cost, with no mandatory platform fees and no features locked behind a paywall. Every install comes with a 12-month hardware warranty and a 12-month workmanship warranty. The only recurring option is the optional Priority Support plan at $30/month plus GST, for faster response, remote diagnostics and hands-on configuration help. Cancel any time.

Are there any hidden fees?

Prices on this site are installed prices in Canadian dollars, before GST, and GST is added to your invoice. Installation, hardware, configuration and training are included in the quoted price. The only optional recurring add-on is the Priority Support plan at $30/month plus GST — no contract, cancel anytime.

What's included in the installation fee?

Installation includes: hardware purchase, professional setup, Z-Wave hub configuration, device pairing, dashboard customization, scene setup, training, and full handoff. Everything needed to go from zero to fully operational.

What about device costs beyond the quoted hardware?

The quoted price includes hardware. If you want to expand beyond the base package, your installer will quote additional devices. You control what gets installed and when.

Is the support plan really optional?

Yes, completely optional. Every install comes with a 12-month hardware warranty and a 12-month workmanship warranty. The Priority Support plan is a $30/month plus GST add-on for homeowners who want priority response and hands-on help configuring new devices or automations later. Add it anytime, cancel anytime.

What warranty comes with an install?

Every install comes with a 12-month hardware warranty and a 12-month workmanship warranty. The hardware warranty covers a device that fails because of a manufacturing defect. The workmanship warranty covers the installation itself: if something was mounted, wired or configured incorrectly on our end, we come back and put it right at no charge. Details are on the warranty and support page and in the Terms of Sale.

Are software updates free?

Software updates are free while your hub is under a current EJG-OS release. We tell you in writing if a version stops being supported. Updates are digitally signed.

Internet, privacy and access

Does EJG-OS work if my internet goes down?

Yes. EJG-OS runs on a Raspberry Pi server inside your home. Your lights, locks, thermostats, scenes, and automations all continue working without any internet connection, from the wall tablet or the app on your home network, and cameras keep recording to the recorder in your home. Skip, the voice assistant, keeps working too. Features that need internet: remote access from your phone when you're away, phone notifications, the optional weather and music extras, and remote diagnostics if you subscribe to Priority Support.

Is anything stored in the cloud?

Your cameras, recordings, device history and settings are stored on the hub in your home, not in the cloud. We don't keep copies on our servers. Two things to know: phone alerts are delivered through Apple, Google and Expo, so the alert text passes through them; and optional services you turn on, such as Spotify or weather, talk to their own providers. An encrypted off-site backup is your choice, and goes only to a device you or your installer control. Installers' own business accounts are separate from your home. Our privacy policy names every service.

Is my data private?

We never sell your data. Your device history, scenes and automations live on the Raspberry Pi server inside your home, not on our servers. Skip, the voice assistant, runs on the hub, and nothing you say to it is sent to us or to any AI service. A few features use outside services: phone alerts pass through Apple and Google as a plain “you have a new alert” notice, with the details opened from your hub in the app; remote access runs through EJG Secure Link, where a small EJG-run service and relay pass encrypted data they cannot read; and the optional weather and music extras, off until you turn them on, use outside services. Every service is named in our privacy policy.

How does remote access work?

Remote access uses EJG Secure Link, which is included in the Base Package. Your phone connects to your hub over a link that is encrypted end to end. EJG runs a small coordination service that helps your devices find each other and a relay that passes encrypted data it cannot read. Remote access needs the internet; everything at home keeps working without it.

What do phone alerts show?

Phone alerts pass through Expo, Apple and Google as a plain "you have a new alert" notice. The details open from your hub in the app. Alerts need the internet to reach your phone.

Who can access my home?

You control it. Two-step verification is available, and the owner can see every trusted device and remove any of them. Using the installer tools on your home needs your approval in the app. Your installer also holds a maintenance key and your hub's secret, used to deliver signed software updates, receive your hub's health report, and service the hub on site.

What happens if my installer leaves the business?

You own all your hardware outright, and your EJG-OS system runs locally on the hub in your house. If we stop being the ones who service it, your lights, locks, scenes and automations keep working exactly as configured. Services we operate — remote access and software updates — would stop until another provider took them over. Your devices are standard Z-Wave, so they can be re-paired to another Z-Wave system if you ever want to.

Skip, the voice assistant

Does it work with voice assistants like Alexa or Google?

EJG-OS includes our own “Hey Skip” voice assistant instead. Skip runs on your hub. It understands a defined set of home commands — lights, locks, the thermostat, scenes, timers, reminders, lists, family messages and more — and answers with a voice generated on the hub. It is not a general chatbot, and it is still being tuned. On the wall tablet, the hub hears the wake word and turns speech into text; on iPhone, speech is turned into text on the phone itself, in English, on phones that have the on-device speech model installed. Nothing you say to Skip is sent to EJG or to any AI service, and Skip on the wall tablet keeps working with the internet unplugged. Skip can be turned off. We don't connect Alexa or Google Home.

What can I say to Skip?

Say "Hey Skip" and then what you want. Skip is the voice assistant built into EJG-OS and runs on your hub. It understands a defined set of home commands: lights, locks, the thermostat, scenes, timers, reminders, lists, family messages and more. It is still being tuned, and unlocking by voice is off unless the owner turns it on.

Devices and your home

What devices are compatible?

EJG-OS supports Z-Wave devices (switches, dimmers, locks, sensors, plugs, thermostats) plus PoE cameras and the indoor camera. We install devices we've tested with EJG-OS, and we confirm compatibility for anything else before you buy. Your installer will recommend specific hardware during the consultation.

What is Z-Wave?

Z-Wave is a smart-home standard with its own low-power radio network between the hub and your devices, separate from your home internet connection. Many manufacturers make Z-Wave devices. A Z-Wave radio connected to your hub talks to them, with S2 encryption on supported devices.

Can I add devices after installation?

Yes. Your installer adds new devices whenever you want them; they pair directly to your local hub, and the work is quoted as service (or covered by the Priority Support plan). In the app you can run scenes, switch automations on or off and manage lock codes; new rooms, scenes and automations are set up by your installer too. EJG-OS software updates are free while your hub is under a current EJG-OS release; we tell you in writing if a version stops being supported. There's no contract locking you into anything.

Can I keep the smart devices I already have?

Z-Wave devices you already own may be able to be paired to your hub, and we confirm compatibility before you buy. Devices that only work through another company's app or hub are not part of EJG-OS. We don't connect Alexa or Google Home.

Is EJG-OS good for renters?

EJG-OS is primarily designed for homeowners. That said, many Z-Wave devices can be installed without permanent modification (especially smart plugs and some light switches). If you're renting, speak to your installer — there may be options that work for your situation. Homeowners with rental properties can also deploy EJG-OS in units they manage.

What if I move?

The hardware becomes yours once you have paid in full, so you can take it with you. Switches, locks and thermostats installed in the walls are set up for your house, and whether they stay or go is your decision. The software is licensed for the hub we install, for your household (see the Terms of Sale), so if you sell the house with the system in it, or want it set up at a new address, contact us first and we will quote the work.

Is EJG-OS an alarm or security system?

EJG-OS is a home automation system: it is not an alarm or monitoring service and does not replace one. It controls lights, locks, heating and scenes, and it can send alerts to you. There is no monitoring centre and nobody is dispatched on your behalf.

Cameras

What do cameras need beyond the cameras themselves?

A dedicated network video recorder (NVR) with its own storage drive. The NVR is required for every camera install: cameras record to it around the clock, and the recordings stay on it, inside your home. There is no cloud video subscription and nobody at EJG watches your cameras. PoE cameras connect to the recorder's built-in PoE ports, which also power them. The indoor pan/tilt camera records to the same NVR. A $300 network switch is offered with any camera build; it powers PoE cameras over the same cables. Past 8 cameras it is required rather than optional, because the recorder runs out of ports, and the builder adds it automatically. You choose the drive size by how many days of footage you want to keep (see the storage table on the pricing page).
