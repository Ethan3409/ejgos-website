Frequently Asked Questions

Getting started, Skip, privacy and internet, pricing and warranty, and cameras — in one place.

Getting started

Do I need to be technical to use it?
No. Everything is set up and configured for you during the install, and tuned on the first day so it works the way your household actually lives. Day to day you use the wall tablet or the EJG Home app: tap a scene, open a lock, check the thermostat. You can run scenes, switch automations on or off and manage lock codes in the app; new devices, rooms, scenes and automations are set up by your installer, so nothing asks you to learn a configuration file.
What devices are compatible?
EJG-OS supports Z-Wave devices (switches, dimmers, locks, sensors, plugs, thermostats) plus PoE cameras and the indoor camera. We install devices we've tested with EJG-OS, and we confirm compatibility for anything else before you buy. Your installer will recommend specific hardware during the consultation.
Can I add devices after installation?
Yes. Your installer adds new devices whenever you want them; they pair directly to your local hub, and the work is quoted as service (or covered by the Priority Support plan). In the app you can run scenes, switch automations on or off and manage lock codes; new rooms, scenes and automations are set up by your installer too. EJG-OS software updates are free. There's no contract locking you into anything.
Is EJG-OS good for renters?
EJG-OS is primarily designed for homeowners. That said, many Z-Wave devices can be installed without permanent modification (especially smart plugs and some light switches). If you're renting, speak to your installer — there may be options that work for your situation. Homeowners with rental properties can also deploy EJG-OS in units they manage.
What happens if my installer leaves the business?
You own all your hardware outright, and your EJG-OS system runs locally on the hub in your house. If we stop being the ones who service it, your lights, locks, scenes and automations keep working exactly as configured. Services we operate — remote access and software updates — would stop until another provider took them over. Your devices are standard Z-Wave, so they can be moved to another system if you ever want to.

Skip, the voice assistant

Does it work with voice assistants like Alexa or Google?
EJG-OS includes our own “Hey Skip” voice assistant instead. Skip runs on your hub. It understands a set of home commands — lights, locks, the thermostat, scenes, timers, reminders, lists, family messages and more — and answers with a voice generated on the hub. On the wall tablet, the hub hears the wake word and turns speech into text; on a phone, speech is turned into text on the phone itself. Nothing you say to Skip is sent to EJG or to any AI service, and Skip on the wall tablet keeps working with the internet unplugged. Skip can be turned off. We don't connect Alexa or Google Home.

Privacy & internet

Does EJG-OS work if my internet goes down?
Yes. EJG-OS runs on a Raspberry Pi server inside your home. Your lights, locks, thermostats, scenes, and automations all continue working without any internet connection, from the wall tablet or the app on your home network, and cameras keep recording to the recorder in your home. Skip, the voice assistant, keeps working too. Features that need internet: remote access from your phone when you're away, phone notifications, the optional weather and music extras, and remote diagnostics if you subscribe to Priority Support.
Is my data private?
We never sell your data. Your device history, scenes and automations live on the Raspberry Pi server inside your home, not on our servers. Skip, the voice assistant, runs on the hub, and nothing you say to it is sent to us or to any AI service. A few features use outside services: phone alerts pass through Apple and Google as a plain “you have a new alert” notice, with the details opened from your hub in the app; remote access runs through Cloudflare or EJG Secure Link; and the optional weather and music extras, off until you turn them on, use outside services. Every service is named in our privacy policy.

Pricing & warranty

Do I need to pay a monthly fee?
No. The base EJG-OS platform is a one-time installation cost. There are no mandatory platform fees, no subscription required to use your smart home, and no features locked behind a paywall. Every install comes with a 12-month hardware warranty and a 12-month workmanship warranty — a Priority Support plan is available at $30/month if you want faster response, remote diagnostics, and hands-on configuration help. Cancel any time.
Are there any hidden fees?
Prices on this site are installed prices in Canadian dollars, before GST, and GST is added to your invoice. Installation, hardware, configuration and training are included in the quoted price. The only optional recurring add-on is the Priority Support plan at $30/month — no contract, cancel anytime.
What's included in the installation fee?
Installation includes: hardware purchase, professional setup, Z-Wave hub configuration, device pairing, dashboard customization, scene setup, training, and full handoff. Everything needed to go from zero to fully operational.
What about device costs beyond the quoted hardware?
The quoted price includes hardware. If you want to expand beyond the base package, your installer will quote additional devices. You control what gets installed and when.
Is the support plan really optional?
Yes, completely optional. Every install comes with a 12-month hardware warranty and a 12-month workmanship warranty. The Priority Support plan is a $30/month add-on for homeowners who want priority response and hands-on help configuring new devices or automations later. Add it anytime, cancel anytime.

Cameras

What do cameras need beyond the cameras themselves?
A dedicated network video recorder (NVR) with its own storage drive. The NVR is required for every camera install: cameras record to it around the clock, and the recordings stay on it, inside your home. PoE cameras plug directly into the NVR's built-in PoE ports, which powers them and keeps them on their own isolated camera network with no direct path to your home network or the internet, and we turn off the recorder's cloud features. The indoor pan/tilt camera records to the same NVR. A $300 network switch is offered with any camera build: it keeps the hub, tablet and indoor camera off the same network as the rest of the house, and powers PoE cameras over the same cables. Past 8 cameras it is required rather than optional, because the recorder runs out of ports, and the builder adds it automatically. You choose the drive size by how many days of footage you want to keep (see the storage table on the pricing page).
