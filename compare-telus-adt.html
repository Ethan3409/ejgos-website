EJG-OS vs Telus and ADT smart home

Telus and EJG-OS are built for different jobs. Telus SmartHome Security is sold with professional monitoring plans. EJG-OS is a home automation system you own, installed in your house. EJG-OS is not an alarm or monitoring service and does not replace one.

Side by side

We compare ownership, local operation, fees, installation, openness and privacy. Statements about Telus SmartHome and ADT Canada are what the company itself publishes, listed under Sources. Plans vary, and details change, so check the company before you buy.

Who owns the hardwareTELUS states that equipment included with its promoted SmartHome Security plans is rental equipment, provided as part of the recurring monthly fee, and must be returned on cancellation. Plans vary.You own the hardware once you have paid in full. The EJG-OS software is licensed for the hub we install, for your household.
Works without the internetThe pages we reviewed do not say.Lights, locks, heating, scenes and automations run on the hub in your home and keep working with the internet unplugged. Remote access, phone alerts and the optional weather and music extras need the internet.
Monthly fees and contractTELUS states that its professionally monitored SmartHome Security plans require a 3-year or 5-year term, with a cancellation fee for early termination. Prices vary by plan and promotion.No required subscription. A one-time installed price (a $1,000 Base Package plus devices at flat installed prices, in CAD before GST). Priority Support is optional at $30/month plus GST, month to month.
InstallationTELUS lists a professional installation fee ($200 on its Control + Video offer page; fees vary by page and promotion) and offers a self-install option.Professionally installed and set up by EJG in Calgary, Airdrie, Cochrane, Okotoks, Chestermere and Springbank.
Platform opennessTELUS's automation page mentions pairing with Amazon Alexa and Siri. We did not find a statement about Z-Wave or other protocols.Z-Wave devices, with S2 encryption on supported devices. EJG-OS is licensed software, not open source. We don't connect Alexa or Google Home.
PrivacyTELUS describes an app-based service. We did not find a statement on where home data is stored. See TELUS's own privacy statements.Your cameras, recordings, device history and settings are stored on the hub in your home, not in the cloud. We don't keep copies on our servers. Phone alerts pass through Apple, Google and Expo, and optional services you turn on use their own providers.

Which fits what

  • If you want a professionally monitored alarm service, TELUS sells one and EJG-OS does not. You can still use both.
  • If you want to own your hub outright, with no required service term, and keep lights, locks and heating working with the internet unplugged, that is what EJG-OS is built for.
