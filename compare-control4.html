EJG-OS vs Control4

Control4 and EJG-OS are both professionally installed home automation systems. The differences below are the ones each company states publicly.

As of October 2026 · Last reviewed October 22, 2026
Home › Compare › Control4

EJG-OS is a home automation system: it is not an alarm or monitoring service and does not replace one.

Side by side

We compare ownership, local operation, fees, installation, openness and privacy. Statements about Control4 are what the company itself publishes, listed under Sources. Plans vary, and details change, so check the company before you buy.

TopicControl4EJG-OS
Who owns the hardwareControl4 documentation says a controller is registered to a Control4 account, which your installer may have set up for you. We did not find a statement about who owns the controller; ask your dealer.You own the hardware once you have paid in full. The EJG-OS software is licensed for the hub we install, for your household.
Works without the internetControl4 documentation says a device on the same local network can control the system. Whether a system works with the internet down is not stated on the pages we reviewed.Lights, locks, heating, scenes and automations run on the hub in your home and keep working with the internet unplugged. Remote access, phone alerts and the optional weather and music extras need the internet.
Monthly fees and contractControl4 documentation says away-from-home control requires an annual 4Sight subscription (the user guide says about $10/month). ADI describes Control4 Connect as an optional service framework for new systems. Plans vary and we did not find Canadian pricing.No required subscription. A one-time installed price (a $1,000 Base Package plus devices at flat installed prices, in CAD before GST). Priority Support is optional at $30/month plus GST, month to month.
InstallationControl4 states that its certified integration professionals install the system.Professionally installed and set up by EJG in Calgary, Airdrie, Cochrane, Okotoks, Chestermere and Springbank.
Platform opennessControl4 states that its systems work with Control4 and third-party products, and its dealer documentation covers Z-Wave and Zigbee.Z-Wave devices, with S2 encryption on supported devices. EJG-OS is licensed software, not open source. We don't connect Alexa or Google Home.
PrivacyWe did not find a Control4 statement on where home data is stored. Ask your dealer and read Control4's privacy statements.Your cameras, recordings, device history and settings are stored on the hub in your home, not in the cloud. We don't keep copies on our servers. Phone alerts pass through Apple, Google and Expo, and optional services you turn on use their own providers.

Which fits what

  • Control4 is sold through its own network of certified dealers. Ask them what their package includes and what it costs.
  • EJG-OS is sold and installed directly by EJG Technologies Inc. in the Calgary area, and the sample packages are priced on our site.
