# EJG-OS wording rules for public copy

One page. Use it for the website, ads, app-store text, quotes, emails and anything else a customer can read. If a claim is not on the "allowed" list, check it against the product first. These rules describe what is true for a new install on current software.

## Never say

| Do not write | Why | Write instead |
|---|---|---|
| "No cloud", "zero cloud", "100% local", "fully offline", "never leaves your home" | Remote access, phone alerts, updates and two optional extras use outside services | "The home runs without the cloud." "Lights, locks, heating and automations keep working with the internet unplugged." |
| "Built local", "Stays local" as a blanket claim | Same reason | "Runs in your home." |
| "Works everywhere", "access from anywhere, always" for remote access | Remote access needs the internet and EJG Secure Link | "Secure remote access from your phone through EJG Secure Link." |
| "Direct connection, no relay", "no servers of ours involved" | Secure Link uses an EJG coordination server and a relay | "EJG runs a small service that helps your devices find each other and a relay that passes encrypted data it cannot read." |
| "Military-grade", "bank-grade", "unhackable", "completely secure" | Not provable | State the specific measure: signed updates, encrypted remote access, owner can remove any device. |
| "Wi-Fi", "wireless internet", or Wi-Fi as an option | EJG-OS is Z-Wave and wired (Ethernet) | Say "Z-Wave" for devices and "wired" for the hub connection. Describe an item by what it does, not its radio. |
| A Skip button, tap-to-talk, push-to-talk, or any non-voice way to trigger Skip | Skip is voice and wake word only | "Say 'Hey Skip'." |
| Skip accuracy or speed numbers ("understands everything", "99%", "instant") | Reliability is still being tuned and has no published test result | "Skip understands a defined set of home commands." |
| "Ask Skip anything", "ask about your home", "AI assistant", "chatbot" | Skip is a preset command engine, not a general chatbot, and sends nothing to an AI service | "Skip is the voice assistant built into EJG-OS. It runs on your hub." |
| "Works with Alexa / Google Home" | Not connected | "We don't connect Alexa or Google Home." |
| A named recorder brand or model | The tested family is not a stated product line | "An 8- or 16-channel PoE network video recorder." |
| "Cameras are isolated from your network / the internet" | Not verified per model and wiring | "Cameras connect to the recorder's PoE ports." |
| "We turn off the recorder's cloud" as a blanket | Only true for a recorder after the hub has set it up | "We set up your recorder to record locally and switch off its vendor remote-viewing service" (only once that step is in the install checklist for that home). |
| "One-visit install" | Homes with cameras take longer | "Most lighting-and-lock installs are done in one day. Homes with cameras take longer; we give you a schedule at the consultation." |
| "Free updates" with no condition | Updates need a supported version | "Software updates are free while your hub is under a current EJG-OS release. We tell you in writing if a version stops being supported." |
| "No monthly fees" | Priority Support is an optional $30/month | "No required subscription." |
| "You own the system" | The software is licensed | "You own the hardware." |
| "Monitored", "24/7 security", "we'll call the police" | EJG-OS is self-monitored | "Self-monitored: alerts come to you. No monitoring centre; nobody is dispatched." |
| "Encrypted on your home network" | Not claimed until proven | Do not claim. |
| "Roadmap", "coming soon", "soon you will be able to" | Sell what exists | Describe only shipped features. |
| "EJG Smart Systems" | Not the legal name | "EJG Technologies Inc." |
| A Priority Support price without "plus GST" | It is taxed | "$30/month plus GST." |

## Allowed claims (true for a new install on current software)

- Lights, locks, heating, scenes and automations are controlled by the hub in your house and keep working with the internet unplugged, from the wall tablet or the app on your home network.
- No required subscription. No cloud account. Priority Support is optional.
- We never sell your data. No ads, no analytics, no trackers on this site or in the apps.
- Your device history, scenes and automations live on the hub in your house.
- Skip runs on your hub; nothing you say to Skip is sent to EJG or any AI service. Skip understands a defined set of home commands and is still being tuned.
- Unlocking by voice is off unless the owner turns it on, and Skip always asks you to confirm.
- Remote access uses EJG Secure Link, encrypted end to end between your phone and your hub.
- Phone alerts pass through Expo, Apple and Google as a plain "you have a new alert" notice; the details open from your hub in the app.
- Software updates are digitally signed.
- Signing in stays signed in on your phone and tablet; two-step verification is available; the owner can see every trusted device and remove any of them; the hub cannot be opened in a web browser.
- Cameras record to a network video recorder in your home. No cloud video subscription. Nobody at EJG watches your cameras.
- Self-monitored security with no monitoring centre.
- Z-Wave devices, with S2 encryption on supported devices.
- A $1,000 base package that always includes three Z-Wave switches. Never offer or imply a bare-bones tier.
- 12-month hardware warranty and 12-month workmanship warranty (see the Terms of Sale).
- Every enquiry answered within one business day.

## Always include

- Prices are in Canadian dollars, before GST.
- Company name: EJG Technologies Inc., Calgary, Alberta.
- For any text or email marketing: the mailing address, a working way to unsubscribe, and consent (CASL).
- Where a claim depends on a condition (current software, a supported device, a recorder set up by us), state the condition.

## Before using a new claim

1. Is it true today, for a new install, without a footnote?
2. Is there a test or a line of code that shows it?
3. Does it name any outside service it relies on?
4. Does it avoid every row of the "never say" table?
