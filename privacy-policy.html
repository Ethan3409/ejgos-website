Privacy Policy

Effective September 6, 2026 Version 1.0

EJG Home is the companion app for EJG-OS, a local-first smart home platform installed by EJG Smart Systems Inc. This policy covers both the EJG Home iPhone app and the EJG Home wall tablet app, and describes exactly what data each handles — and what it deliberately does not.

What EJG Home collectsOnly what's needed to talk to your hub.

  • Login credentials. Username and password (or PIN) are stored in encrypted device storage — iOS Keychain on iPhone, Android's SharedPreferences on the tablet — and used to authenticate with your EJG-OS hub. They are never transmitted to EJG Smart Systems or any third party.
  • Hub address. The Cloudflare Tunnel hostname or local IP of your EJG-OS hub, entered once during setup and stored on your device only.
  • Device identifier. A random per-install ID your hub uses to distinguish this device from any other device signed into the same account. Not shared externally.
  • Push notification token. Issued by Apple Push Notification Service or Firebase Cloud Messaging and sent to your hub so it can deliver alerts about your home. Not shared with third parties.

What EJG Home does not collectNo trackers, no analytics, no advertising.

  • No advertising identifiers, marketing SDKs, or third-party analytics of any kind.
  • No location tracking. Neither app requests GPS access.
  • No access to your contacts, calendar, or photo library.
  • No access to your microphone or camera unless you explicitly grant it for a specific feature. Voice control via Skip requires the microphone; you can revoke that permission at any time in your device's settings.
  • No behavioural telemetry. Neither app reports which screens you visit or how you use it.

Your hub handles the restEverything you see in the app lives on your hub.

Your EJG-OS hub is a physical device installed at your home. It is the source of every reading, event, and recording surfaced in the app. Device state, camera footage, sensor history, and automation logs live on that hub, which is under your physical control.

Nothing is uploaded to EJG Smart Systems servers unless you explicitly enable remote access — via a Cloudflare Tunnel or Tailscale — and even then, the data path is between your hub and your app only.

Voice control (Skip)Audio goes to your hub; language processing may reach OpenAI.

When you invoke voice control, your microphone audio is streamed to your EJG-OS hub for processing. From the hub, the transcript may be sent to OpenAI's API for language understanding, subject to OpenAI's own privacy policy. The transcript is not stored by EJG Smart Systems.

Push notificationsComposed on your hub, delivered by Apple or Google.

Push notification content is composed on your hub and passed through Apple Push Notification Service (iOS) or Firebase Cloud Messaging (Android) to your device. EJG Smart Systems does not read, process, or store the content of these notifications.

Permissions requestedOnly what each feature needs.

  • Network access — to reach your local EJG-OS hub.
  • Microphone — only if you enable Skip voice control.
  • Notifications — only if you opt in when prompted.
  • Wake lock (tablet only) — to keep the wall panel display on.
  • Boot completed (tablet only) — to auto-launch the wall panel after a restart.

Children's privacyNot directed at children under 13.

EJG Home is designed for use by adult homeowners. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

ChangesUpdates will bump the version and date above.

Material changes to this policy will be reflected in an updated version number and effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after such a change constitutes acceptance.
